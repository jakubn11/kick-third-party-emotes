# Kick Third-Party Emotes — Manual Testing

Moved from [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) (**Manual Testing**), which keeps a short index. Background for each case is in [implementation-notes.md](implementation-notes.md).

Manual testing is required in a browser with a userscript manager installed:

1. Install the userscript via your manager (e.g. drag the `.user.js` file into Tampermonkey, Violentmonkey, Greasemonkey, ScriptCat, or other, or copy it into the folder configured in the Userscripts extension on Safari).
2. Open a Kick channel page.
3. Check the browser developer console for `[KickEmotes]` log lines.
4. Verify global and channel emotes render in chat.
5. Verify autocomplete attaches to the chat input and supports arrow navigation, Tab selection, Enter selection (and plain Enter sending with nothing highlighted), and Esc close. Complete a word that **starts** the message (`lo` → `LOL`) as well as one mid-message, then type on: the text continues after the emote. Test Enter selection logged out or in your own channel: with a highlighted suggestion it must insert, never send — a send there is the 2.15.7 bug.
6. Navigate between Kick channels and confirm channel-specific emotes reload.
7. Right-click a rendered emote and verify the context menu (favourite, copy name, copy image URL, open provider page). Do the same on an emote in the picker: choosing an item must leave the picker open. In theatre mode, open the menu and press Esc: the menu closes and the player stays in theatre mode (leaving it is the 2.15.8 bug).
8. Insert a few emotes, reopen the picker's 7TV+ tab, and verify the "Recently used" section and usage-ranked autocomplete.
9. Favourite an emote from chat and from the picker; verify the ★ appears in the tooltip, the autocomplete row, and the picker badge, that the "Favourites" section updates while the picker is open, and that unstarring reverses all of it.
10. Hover an emote in chat and in the picker and verify the tooltip shows the large preview without jumping once the image loads.
11. Hover an emote in a busy chat without moving the cursor: the tooltip should track the emote as messages scroll it up, and disappear once the emote scrolls out or its row is recycled.
12. Toggle a provider chip off and on in the picker: its section and emotes should vanish from the picker, autocomplete, and *already-rendered* chat messages, and come back intact. Toggle repeatedly and confirm no spaces accumulate around zero-width emotes.
13. In a provider group with more than 40 emotes, click **Load more** twice, then **Show less**: the group goes back to 40 with only **Load more** showing, the count reads `Showing 40 of …`, the footer stays in view, and Load more still works afterwards. Expand a group again, then star an emote from its right-click menu: the group keeps its count and the view doesn't jump. Type a search and clear it: groups start at 40 again.
14. Scroll the picker grid and confirm the settings chip row hides, then scroll back to the top and confirm it returns. Check both scroll cases: the tab's own scrollbar, and a short tab where Kick's panel is the scroller.
15. Open `/popout/<channel>/chat` and confirm channel emotes load, not just globals.
16. Post `cvMask` after another emote in a channel with BTTV loaded and verify it overlays rather than sitting beside it.
17. Compare the 7TV+ tab button against Kick's native tabs: same size and spacing, the active underline appears under it when the tab is selected, no horizontal scrollbar shows under the tab strip, and the grey line under the tabs runs under the 7TV+ tab too. Narrow the window until the strip scrolls sideways, scroll it to the end, and check the line still reaches the last tab.
18. Open a channel whose URL has a dash where the username has an underscore (e.g. `/xqc-lobotomy`) and confirm its 7TV channel emotes load. Open one with Twitch BTTV emotes (e.g. `/trainwreckstv`) and confirm BTTV channel emotes load, tagged `BTTV (twitch)`.
19. Enable a **second** copy of the script (a duplicate entry in the same manager, or a second manager) and reload. The console must show one `[KickEmotes] v… active` line and one `… is standing down` warning naming both versions; chat, the picker and autocomplete behave exactly as with one copy.
20. Turn on the system's **Reduce motion** setting with a channel open (macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Display). Without a reload, animated 7TV and FFZ emotes in chat, autocomplete, the hover preview and the picker should freeze on their still frame, the picker should stop animating on hover, and turning the setting off should bring the animation back. Animated BTTV emotes keep moving.
21. Signed in, open Kick's chat settings (the gear beside **Chat**): **Third-party emotes** is the last row, at Kick's row height but in the family look (the add-on rows share a dark card set apart from Kick's rows, with the green tile, a green hover and focus ring), and comes back after visiting a sub-page and returning. Open the menu **before** the script loads (or reload with it open, for a manager that injects late): the row is there straight away, once. Clicking it closes Kick's menu and opens the settings panel — check this on a wide window (popover) and a narrow one, around 500px wide (drawer), and that the page is clickable again after closing the panel. Open the panel from the manager menu too, where it has one. Make the window short enough that the panel scrolls and check, in Safari as well, that the last group's **Clear recently used** button sits inside its group and that the gaps left and right of the groups match. Turn one provider's **Global emotes** off: its global-only emotes leave chat, autocomplete and the picker while its channel emotes stay. Set **Animated emotes** to *On hover* and check a 7TV emote (and an overlay on it) is still until hovered, then *Never* and check the hover preview stays still too. Switch **Suggest emotes while typing** off and confirm Tab and Enter behave as without the script. Switch **Emote size** through Small/Large: chat emotes and overlays resize in place. Turn **Large preview on hover** off: hovering shows only the label. The *Recently used* group shows the same emotes as the picker's section, starred ones with a ★; hovering one shows the tooltip above the panel. **Clear recently used** asks once more, then empties the picker's *Recently used* section and the panel's emotes, and leaves favourites. Reload: every choice is kept. With a second Kick tab open, change a setting, star an emote and clear recents in one: the other follows without a reload, and inserting an emote there does not bring the cleared recents back. Esc and a click outside close the panel; Tab from the last control wraps to the first, and Shift+Tab from the first to the last. With the keyboard only, Tab to a size chip and press Space, then flip a switch, then arm, cancel and confirm **Clear recently used**: focus stays on the control you pressed (or moves to Clear or Close once that control is gone) and the next Tab stays inside the panel.
